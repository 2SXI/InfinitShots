About the studio

A boutique studio, on purpose.

Infinit Shots is run by Tshepo, based in Johannesburg. Boutique isn't a marketing word here — it means a small number of bookings, handled personally, rather than a big team pushing volume.

Every flight is planned before it happens: the shot list, the light, the airspace. That's what separates footage that looks intentional from footage that just happens to be high up.
